# Academia DZ — Payment Conversion Analysis

![Dashboard Screenshot](اسم_الصورة.png)

## Key Insight
Only 18.9% of transactions completed payment successfully

Card 1 — 💳 Payment Conversion is the Core Problem
More than 4 out of 5 payment attempts fail (81.1%). This is the single biggest issue in the funnel and the top priority for further investigation.
The problem I found is that e-payment in Algeria is not common, and that is why there are many failed transactions, many students did not trust the site to pay their money, so I had to gain their trust first, and this is what I worked on, which resulted in 28 successful operations.

Card 2 — 🎓 License vs. Master's Split
License students account for 62.8% of transactions vs. 37.2% for Master's — but payment success rate per level hasn't been isolated yet. Open question worth answering next.

Card 3 — 📉 Volume Dropped Sharply (April → June)
Transaction volume declined significantly across the tracked months. This is because Academia DZ is a website for generating university research, so it is reasonable to assume that there will be a peak in the months of study, knowing that the site was launched in January and the first advertising campaign was launched in February as April was the peak and then the decline started as the academic year ended.

Card — 📚 Payment Behavior Varies by Field
Payment activity is concentrated in a few academic fields, with الحقوق والعلوم السياسية and العلوم الاجتماعية leading, while others show minimal paid volume — a signal for where marketing effort could focus.
